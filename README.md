## Selected product work

### Enterprise Spend Management — FARFETCH

Product Manager for the LATAM Business Spend Management platform, supporting implementation in Brazil and Mexico. Scope included request-to-pay workflows, Delegation of Authority approvals, tax and Accounts Payable needs, and integrations with Microsoft D365, Power BI, and Amazon PunchOut. I coordinated requirements and delivery dependencies across Procurement, Finance, Legal, HR, IT, and Operations in a SOX context.

[Read the Coupa case study](https://github.com/alvaroadriano/product-management-portfolio/blob/main/coupa-business-spend-management.md)

### Marketplace and E-commerce — Violet Grey / FARFETCH

Owned product strategy, roadmap, requirements, user stories, and backlog priorities for connecting Violet Grey’s beauty catalog to the FARFETCH marketplace while supporting its standalone storefront. The work included personalized recommendations and bundled purchases. A product rule was to show the recommendation module only when a relevant recommendation was available. Initial conversions occurred; no quantified uplift is claimed.

[Read the marketplace case study](https://github.com/alvaroadriano/product-management-portfolio/blob/main/marketplace-ecommerce-product-management.md)

### Omnichannel Web Sales — X7 Brasil

Developed product strategy and roadmap for web sales applications supporting online, mobile, social, email, and in-store channels. I used business priorities, user feedback, and research to shape the roadmap, prioritized the backlog, and presented plans to executives. I established and developed the Omnichannel Marketing department. No verified sales or adoption metrics are available for this product case.
